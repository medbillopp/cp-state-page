## MEDICAID ENROLLMENT SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: medicaid-enrollment
H2: Medicaid Enrollment
INTRO: Montana Medicaid is administered by the Montana Department of Public Health and Human Services (DPHHS) through its Montana Healthcare Programs division. Montana expanded Medicaid under the HELP Plan (Health and Economic Livelihood Partnership) and delivers most Medicaid services through a fee-for-service model, with care coordination provided through Primary Care Case Management and the Montana Behavioral Health system. There is no single national portal. Providers must complete enrollment directly through DPHHS before they can bill for services rendered to Medicaid recipients in the state. Federal law requires that physicians and other practitioners who prescribe, order, refer, or bill services for Montana Medicaid recipients be enrolled as Medicaid providers. This applies to both individual providers and group practices.

SUB_1_H3: How Montana Medicaid Provider Enrollment Works
SUB_1_BODY: When a provider enrolls with DPHHS, they are registering at the state Medicaid level. Montana primarily uses fee-for-service billing for most physical health Medicaid services, which means state enrollment is the central step for most provider types. Behavioral health providers may have additional enrollment requirements through Montana's Behavioral Health managed care system. Our team identifies any additional steps required based on your specialty at the start of every engagement.

SUB_2_H3: Enrollment Type Selection
SUB_2_BODY: Choosing the wrong enrollment type is the most common mistake Montana providers make when attempting to enroll on their own. DPHHS requires providers to select the correct enrollment category based on provider type, specialty, and billing structure. An incorrect selection results in a denial that requires starting the process over. Our team confirms the correct enrollment type before any application is submitted.

SUB_3_H3: CAQH ProView and Montana Medicaid
SUB_3_BODY: DPHHS interfaces with CAQH ProView as a primary source for provider data. An expired CAQH attestation, which requires re-attestation every 120 days, can trigger rejection of a pending Montana Medicaid application. Contracting Providers monitors attestation schedules for every provider we work with to prevent this from happening.

SUB_4_H3: Revalidation Requirements
SUB_4_BODY: Federal law requires Montana Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment periodically. Missing a revalidation deadline results in automatic disenrollment. The provider can no longer bill Medicaid until re-enrollment is complete. Our team tracks revalidation dates and initiates the process in advance of deadlines to prevent any gap in enrollment status.

SUB_5_H3: Payer-Specific Nuances

PAYER_1_H4: Montana Medicaid (DPHHS)
PAYER_1_BODY: Enrollment is completed through the DPHHS provider enrollment portal for Montana Healthcare Programs. Montana primarily uses fee-for-service billing for most physical health services, making state enrollment the central requirement for most providers. Providers must select the correct enrollment type before submission. An incorrect selection results in a denial and requires restarting the process.

PAYER_2_H4: Montana Behavioral Health Managed Care
PAYER_2_BODY: Montana manages behavioral health services separately from physical health. Behavioral health providers, including licensed counselors, psychiatrists, and substance use treatment providers, may need to complete additional enrollment steps through Montana's Behavioral Health managed care system after state enrollment is confirmed. Contracting Providers identifies applicable requirements at intake based on the provider's specialty.

PAYER_3_H4: NPI and Taxonomy Requirements
PAYER_3_BODY: Every DPHHS application requires a valid National Provider Identifier. For group practices, a Type 2 NPI is required in addition to each rendering provider's Type 1. Taxonomy codes must accurately reflect the provider's specialty. Mismatched taxonomy codes are a common cause of rejections and can set a provider back four to six weeks.

PAYER_4_H4: Disclaimer
PAYER_4_BODY: Contracting Providers is not DPHHS or any Montana state agency. We help healthcare providers prepare, manage, and complete payer enrollment and credentialing tasks, including Montana Medicaid provider enrollment support. DPHHS controls all final enrollment decisions and approvals.

## PROVIDER CREDENTIALING SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: provider-credentialing
H2: Provider Credentialing

PARAGRAPH_1: Montana Medicaid provider enrollment and provider credentialing are closely linked but distinct processes. Enrollment grants a provider the right to bill DPHHS for Montana Medicaid claims. Credentialing verifies that the provider meets the clinical and professional standards required by DPHHS and any managed care entities serving Montana Medicaid beneficiaries.
PARAGRAPH_2: For behavioral health providers participating in Montana's managed behavioral health system, credentialing may be required as a condition of network participation. Physical health providers billing through fee-for-service typically need only state enrollment and current CAQH data. Our team identifies the right path based on your specialty.

INVOLVES_H3: What Credentialing Involves
INVOLVES_ITEM_1: Primary source verification of Montana medical licenses, DEA certificates, and board certifications
INVOLVES_ITEM_2: Review of malpractice history and National Practitioner Data Bank queries
INVOLVES_ITEM_3: Hospital affiliation verification where applicable
INVOLVES_ITEM_4: CAQH ProView data review and completion
INVOLVES_ITEM_5: Behavioral health managed care entity credentialing review where applicable

TIMELINES_H3: Credentialing Timelines
TIMELINES_BODY: Credentialing through Montana managed care entities, where applicable, typically takes 60 to 120 days from initial application submission. Delays occur most commonly due to incomplete applications, outdated CAQH data, or unresolved primary source verification issues. Contracting Providers front-loads the process with a pre-submission audit to minimize avoidable delays.

BILLING_READINESS_H3: Credentialing and Billing Readiness
BILLING_READINESS_BODY: A provider who completes state enrollment but has not yet completed any required managed care credentialing may face a gap period during which certain claims are denied. Our team coordinates enrollment and credentialing timelines simultaneously to reduce or eliminate that gap.
