## MEDICAID ENROLLMENT SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: medicaid-enrollment
H2: Medicaid Enrollment
INTRO: Missouri Medicaid, known as MO HealthNet, is administered by the MO HealthNet Division of the Missouri Department of Social Services, a state agency that operates independently of the federal Medicaid framework while following CMS guidelines. There is no single national portal. Providers must complete enrollment directly through MO HealthNet before they can bill for services rendered to Medicaid recipients in the state. Missouri delivers most Medicaid services through managed care, which means providers must complete both state enrollment and managed care plan credentialing for full billing access. Federal law requires that physicians and other practitioners who prescribe, order, refer, or bill services for Missouri Medicaid recipients be enrolled as Medicaid providers. This applies to both individual providers and group practices.

SUB_1_H3: How Missouri Medicaid Provider Enrollment Works
SUB_1_BODY: When a provider enrolls with MO HealthNet, they are registering at the state Medicaid level. This is the foundational enrollment step. After state enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to participate with Missouri Medicaid managed care plans must complete a separate credentialing and enrollment process with each plan individually. These are two distinct processes, and managed care plans will not begin their review until the state enrollment is confirmed.

SUB_2_H3: Enrollment Type Selection
SUB_2_BODY: Choosing the wrong enrollment type is the most common mistake Missouri providers make when attempting to enroll on their own. MO HealthNet requires providers to select the correct enrollment category based on provider type, specialty, and billing structure. An incorrect selection results in a denial that requires starting the process over. Our team confirms the correct enrollment type before any application is submitted.

SUB_3_H3: CAQH ProView and Missouri Medicaid
SUB_3_BODY: MO HealthNet and its managed care organizations interface with CAQH ProView as a primary source for provider data. An expired CAQH attestation, which requires re-attestation every 120 days, can trigger rejection of a pending Missouri Medicaid application. Contracting Providers monitors attestation schedules for every provider we work with to prevent this from happening.

SUB_4_H3: Revalidation Requirements
SUB_4_BODY: Federal law requires Missouri Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment periodically. Missing a revalidation deadline results in automatic disenrollment. The provider can no longer bill Medicaid until re-enrollment is complete. Our team tracks revalidation dates and initiates the process in advance of deadlines to prevent any gap in enrollment status.

SUB_5_H3: Payer-Specific Nuances

PAYER_1_H4: MO HealthNet (Missouri Department of Social Services)
PAYER_1_BODY: Enrollment is completed through the MO HealthNet provider enrollment portal maintained by the Missouri Department of Social Services. MO HealthNet is Missouri's Medicaid program name and operates primarily through managed care. Providers must select the correct enrollment type before submission. An incorrect selection results in a denial and requires restarting the process.

PAYER_2_H4: MO HealthNet Managed Care Plans
PAYER_2_BODY: After MO HealthNet enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to bill through Missouri Medicaid managed care plans must enroll separately with each plan operating in the state, including Anthem, HomeState Health (Centene), Missouri Care, and UnitedHealthcare Community Plan. These enrollments run on independent timelines and involve their own credentialing processes. Contracting Providers coordinates managed care enrollment following MO HealthNet confirmation so providers achieve full billing access as efficiently as possible.

PAYER_3_H4: NPI and Taxonomy Requirements
PAYER_3_BODY: Every MO HealthNet application requires a valid National Provider Identifier. For group practices, a Type 2 NPI is required in addition to each rendering provider's Type 1. Taxonomy codes must accurately reflect the provider's specialty. Mismatched taxonomy codes are a common cause of rejections and can set a provider back four to six weeks.

PAYER_4_H4: Disclaimer
PAYER_4_BODY: Contracting Providers is not MO HealthNet or any Missouri state agency. We help healthcare providers prepare, manage, and complete payer enrollment and credentialing tasks, including Missouri Medicaid provider enrollment support. MO HealthNet controls all final enrollment decisions and approvals.

## PROVIDER CREDENTIALING SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: provider-credentialing
H2: Provider Credentialing

PARAGRAPH_1: Missouri Medicaid provider enrollment and provider credentialing are closely linked but distinct processes. Enrollment grants a provider the right to bill MO HealthNet for Missouri Medicaid claims. Credentialing verifies that the provider meets the clinical and professional standards required by MO HealthNet and its managed care organizations.
PARAGRAPH_2: Most MO HealthNet managed care plans require credentialing as a condition of in-network participation, even after state enrollment is complete. Without credentialing, a provider may be enrolled at the state level but unable to bill through the managed care plans that serve the majority of Missouri Medicaid beneficiaries.

INVOLVES_H3: What Credentialing Involves
INVOLVES_ITEM_1: Primary source verification of Missouri medical licenses, DEA certificates, and board certifications
INVOLVES_ITEM_2: Review of malpractice history and National Practitioner Data Bank queries
INVOLVES_ITEM_3: Hospital affiliation verification where applicable
INVOLVES_ITEM_4: CAQH ProView data review and completion
INVOLVES_ITEM_5: MO HealthNet managed care plan credentialing committee review and approval

TIMELINES_H3: Credentialing Timelines
TIMELINES_BODY: Credentialing through a MO HealthNet managed care plan typically takes 60 to 120 days from initial application submission. Delays occur most commonly due to incomplete applications, outdated CAQH data, or unresolved primary source verification issues. Contracting Providers front-loads the process with a pre-submission audit to minimize avoidable delays.

BILLING_READINESS_H3: Credentialing and Billing Readiness
BILLING_READINESS_BODY: A provider who completes MO HealthNet enrollment but has not yet been credentialed by the relevant managed care plans may face a gap period during which claims are denied. Our team coordinates enrollment and credentialing timelines simultaneously to reduce or eliminate that gap.
