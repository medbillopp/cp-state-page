## MEDICAID ENROLLMENT SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: medicaid-enrollment
H2: Medicaid Enrollment
INTRO: Oklahoma Medicaid, known as SoonerCare, is administered by the Oklahoma Health Care Authority (OHCA), a state agency that operates independently of the federal Medicaid framework while following CMS guidelines. There is no single national portal. Providers must complete enrollment directly through OHCA before they can bill for services rendered to Medicaid recipients in the state. Oklahoma has transitioned to a managed care delivery model through SoonerSelect, which means most SoonerCare beneficiaries now receive services through SoonerSelect managed care plans. Federal law requires that physicians and other practitioners who prescribe, order, refer, or bill services for Oklahoma Medicaid recipients be enrolled as Medicaid providers. This applies to both individual providers and group practices.

SUB_1_H3: How Oklahoma Medicaid Provider Enrollment Works
SUB_1_BODY: When a provider enrolls with OHCA, they are registering at the state SoonerCare level. This is the foundational enrollment step. After OHCA enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to participate with SoonerSelect managed care plans must complete a separate credentialing and enrollment process with each plan. These are two distinct processes, and SoonerSelect plans will not begin their review until OHCA enrollment is confirmed.

SUB_2_H3: Enrollment Type Selection
SUB_2_BODY: Choosing the wrong enrollment type is the most common mistake Oklahoma providers make when attempting to enroll on their own. OHCA requires providers to select the correct enrollment category based on provider type, specialty, and billing structure. An incorrect selection results in a denial that requires starting the process over. Our team confirms the correct enrollment type before any application is submitted.

SUB_3_H3: CAQH ProView and Oklahoma Medicaid
SUB_3_BODY: OHCA and its SoonerSelect managed care plans interface with CAQH ProView as a primary source for provider data. An expired CAQH attestation, which requires re-attestation every 120 days, can trigger rejection of a pending Oklahoma Medicaid application. Contracting Providers monitors attestation schedules for every provider we work with to prevent this from happening.

SUB_4_H3: Revalidation Requirements
SUB_4_BODY: Federal law requires Oklahoma Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment periodically. Missing a revalidation deadline results in automatic disenrollment. The provider can no longer bill Medicaid until re-enrollment is complete. Our team tracks revalidation dates and initiates the process in advance of deadlines to prevent any gap in enrollment status.

SUB_5_H3: Payer-Specific Nuances

PAYER_1_H4: SoonerCare (Oklahoma Health Care Authority)
PAYER_1_BODY: Enrollment is completed through the OHCA provider enrollment portal. SoonerCare is Oklahoma's branded Medicaid program and operates through the Oklahoma Health Care Authority. Providers must select the correct enrollment type before submission. An incorrect selection results in a denial and requires restarting the process.

PAYER_2_H4: SoonerSelect Managed Care Plans
PAYER_2_BODY: After OHCA enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to bill through Oklahoma's SoonerSelect managed care program must enroll separately with each SoonerSelect plan operating in the state, including Humana Healthy Horizons of Oklahoma and Aetna Better Health of Oklahoma. These enrollments run on independent timelines and involve their own credentialing processes. Contracting Providers coordinates SoonerSelect plan enrollment following OHCA confirmation so providers achieve full billing access as efficiently as possible.

PAYER_3_H4: NPI and Taxonomy Requirements
PAYER_3_BODY: Every OHCA application requires a valid National Provider Identifier. For group practices, a Type 2 NPI is required in addition to each rendering provider's Type 1. Taxonomy codes must accurately reflect the provider's specialty. Mismatched taxonomy codes are a common cause of rejections and can set a provider back four to six weeks.

PAYER_4_H4: Disclaimer
PAYER_4_BODY: Contracting Providers is not OHCA or any Oklahoma state agency. We help healthcare providers prepare, manage, and complete payer enrollment and credentialing tasks, including Oklahoma Medicaid provider enrollment support. OHCA controls all final enrollment decisions and approvals.

## PROVIDER CREDENTIALING SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: provider-credentialing
H2: Provider Credentialing

PARAGRAPH_1: Oklahoma Medicaid provider enrollment and provider credentialing are closely linked but distinct processes. Enrollment grants a provider the right to bill OHCA for SoonerCare claims. Credentialing verifies that the provider meets the clinical and professional standards required by OHCA and its SoonerSelect managed care plans.
PARAGRAPH_2: SoonerSelect managed care plans require credentialing as a condition of in-network participation, even after OHCA enrollment is complete. Without credentialing, a provider may be enrolled at the state level but unable to bill through the SoonerSelect plans that serve the majority of Oklahoma Medicaid beneficiaries.

INVOLVES_H3: What Credentialing Involves
INVOLVES_ITEM_1: Primary source verification of Oklahoma medical licenses, DEA certificates, and board certifications
INVOLVES_ITEM_2: Review of malpractice history and National Practitioner Data Bank queries
INVOLVES_ITEM_3: Hospital affiliation verification where applicable
INVOLVES_ITEM_4: CAQH ProView data review and completion
INVOLVES_ITEM_5: SoonerSelect managed care plan credentialing committee review and approval

TIMELINES_H3: Credentialing Timelines
TIMELINES_BODY: Credentialing through a SoonerSelect managed care plan typically takes 60 to 120 days from initial application submission. Delays occur most commonly due to incomplete applications, outdated CAQH data, or unresolved primary source verification issues. Contracting Providers front-loads the process with a pre-submission audit to minimize avoidable delays.

BILLING_READINESS_H3: Credentialing and Billing Readiness
BILLING_READINESS_BODY: A provider who completes OHCA enrollment but has not yet been credentialed by the relevant SoonerSelect plans may face a gap period during which claims are denied. Our team coordinates enrollment and credentialing timelines simultaneously to reduce or eliminate that gap.
