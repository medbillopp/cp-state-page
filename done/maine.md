## MEDICAID ENROLLMENT SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: medicaid-enrollment
H2: Medicaid Enrollment
INTRO: Maine Medicaid, known as MaineCare, is administered by the Maine Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), a state agency that operates independently of the federal Medicaid framework while following CMS guidelines. There is no single national portal. Providers must complete enrollment directly through Maine DHHS before they can bill for services rendered to MaineCare recipients in the state. Maine has been expanding its managed care delivery model, and providers should confirm the current delivery structure applicable to their specialty with our team at intake. Federal law requires that physicians and other practitioners who prescribe, order, refer, or bill services for Maine Medicaid recipients be enrolled as Medicaid providers. This applies to both individual providers and group practices.

SUB_1_H3: How MaineCare Provider Enrollment Works
SUB_1_BODY: When a provider enrolls with Maine DHHS, they are registering at the state MaineCare level. This is the foundational enrollment step. Providers serving populations covered by MaineCare managed care plans must complete a separate credentialing and enrollment process with those plans after state enrollment is confirmed. Our team identifies the current managed care enrollment requirements applicable to your specialty and service area at the start of every engagement.

SUB_2_H3: Enrollment Type Selection
SUB_2_BODY: Choosing the wrong enrollment type is the most common mistake Maine providers make when attempting to enroll on their own. Maine DHHS requires providers to select the correct enrollment category based on provider type, specialty, and billing structure. An incorrect selection results in a denial that requires starting the process over. Our team confirms the correct enrollment type before any application is submitted.

SUB_3_H3: CAQH ProView and MaineCare
SUB_3_BODY: Maine DHHS and MaineCare managed care plans interface with CAQH ProView as a primary source for provider data. An expired CAQH attestation, which requires re-attestation every 120 days, can trigger rejection of a pending MaineCare application. Contracting Providers monitors attestation schedules for every provider we work with to prevent this from happening.

SUB_4_H3: Revalidation Requirements
SUB_4_BODY: Federal law requires Maine Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment periodically. Missing a revalidation deadline results in automatic disenrollment. The provider can no longer bill Medicaid until re-enrollment is complete. Our team tracks revalidation dates and initiates the process in advance of deadlines to prevent any gap in enrollment status.

SUB_5_H3: Payer-Specific Nuances

PAYER_1_H4: MaineCare (Maine Department of Health and Human Services)
PAYER_1_BODY: Enrollment is completed through the MaineCare provider enrollment portal maintained by Maine DHHS. Providers must select the correct enrollment type before submission. An incorrect selection results in a denial and requires restarting the process.

PAYER_2_H4: MaineCare Managed Care Plans
PAYER_2_BODY: Maine has been expanding managed care delivery within MaineCare. Providers serving populations enrolled in managed care plans must complete a separate enrollment and credentialing process with those plans after state enrollment is confirmed. Contracting Providers identifies applicable managed care requirements based on specialty and service area, and coordinates managed care enrollment following MaineCare state confirmation.

PAYER_3_H4: NPI and Taxonomy Requirements
PAYER_3_BODY: Every MaineCare application requires a valid National Provider Identifier. For group practices, a Type 2 NPI is required in addition to each rendering provider's Type 1. Taxonomy codes must accurately reflect the provider's specialty. Mismatched taxonomy codes are a common cause of rejections and can set a provider back four to six weeks.

PAYER_4_H4: Disclaimer
PAYER_4_BODY: Contracting Providers is not Maine DHHS or any Maine state agency. We help healthcare providers prepare, manage, and complete payer enrollment and credentialing tasks, including MaineCare provider enrollment support. Maine DHHS controls all final enrollment decisions and approvals.

## PROVIDER CREDENTIALING SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: provider-credentialing
H2: Provider Credentialing

PARAGRAPH_1: Maine Medicaid provider enrollment and provider credentialing are closely linked but distinct processes. Enrollment grants a provider the right to bill Maine DHHS for MaineCare claims. Credentialing verifies that the provider meets the clinical and professional standards required by Maine DHHS and any MaineCare managed care plans applicable to their specialty.
PARAGRAPH_2: Providers participating in MaineCare managed care may require credentialing as a condition of network participation in addition to state enrollment. Our team identifies current credentialing requirements based on the provider's specialty and billing pathway.

INVOLVES_H3: What Credentialing Involves
INVOLVES_ITEM_1: Primary source verification of Maine medical licenses, DEA certificates, and board certifications
INVOLVES_ITEM_2: Review of malpractice history and National Practitioner Data Bank queries
INVOLVES_ITEM_3: Hospital affiliation verification where applicable
INVOLVES_ITEM_4: CAQH ProView data review and completion
INVOLVES_ITEM_5: MaineCare managed care plan credentialing committee review and approval where applicable

TIMELINES_H3: Credentialing Timelines
TIMELINES_BODY: Credentialing through MaineCare managed care plans, where applicable, typically takes 60 to 120 days from initial application submission. Delays occur most commonly due to incomplete applications, outdated CAQH data, or unresolved primary source verification issues. Contracting Providers front-loads the process with a pre-submission audit to minimize avoidable delays.

BILLING_READINESS_H3: Credentialing and Billing Readiness
BILLING_READINESS_BODY: A provider who completes state enrollment but has not yet completed any required managed care credentialing may face a gap period during which certain claims are denied. Our team coordinates enrollment and credentialing timelines simultaneously to reduce or eliminate that gap.
