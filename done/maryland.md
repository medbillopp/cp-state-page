## MEDICAID ENROLLMENT SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: medicaid-enrollment
H2: Medicaid Enrollment
INTRO: Maryland Medicaid, administered through the Maryland Department of Health (MDH) Medical Assistance Program, operates primarily through HealthChoice, Maryland's managed care Medicaid program. HealthChoice is one of the oldest Medicaid managed care programs in the country and serves the majority of Maryland Medicaid beneficiaries. There is no single national portal. Providers must complete enrollment directly through MDH before they can bill for services rendered to Medicaid recipients in the state. Because HealthChoice is a managed care program, providers must also enroll with each HealthChoice MCO to achieve full billing access. Federal law requires that physicians and other practitioners who prescribe, order, refer, or bill services for Maryland Medicaid recipients be enrolled as Medicaid providers. This applies to both individual providers and group practices.

SUB_1_H3: How Maryland Medicaid Provider Enrollment Works
SUB_1_BODY: When a provider enrolls with MDH, they are registering at the state Medical Assistance level. This is the foundational enrollment step. After state enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to participate with HealthChoice managed care plans must complete a separate credentialing and enrollment process with each plan. These are two distinct processes, and HealthChoice MCOs will not begin their review until state enrollment is confirmed.

SUB_2_H3: Enrollment Type Selection
SUB_2_BODY: Choosing the wrong enrollment type is the most common mistake Maryland providers make when attempting to enroll on their own. MDH requires providers to select the correct enrollment category based on provider type, specialty, and billing structure. An incorrect selection results in a denial that requires starting the process over. Our team confirms the correct enrollment type before any application is submitted.

SUB_3_H3: CAQH ProView and Maryland Medicaid
SUB_3_BODY: MDH and its HealthChoice managed care plans interface with CAQH ProView as a primary source for provider data. An expired CAQH attestation, which requires re-attestation every 120 days, can trigger rejection of a pending Maryland Medical Assistance application. Contracting Providers monitors attestation schedules for every provider we work with to prevent this from happening.

SUB_4_H3: Revalidation Requirements
SUB_4_BODY: Federal law requires Maryland Medicaid providers to revalidate their enrollment periodically. Missing a revalidation deadline results in automatic disenrollment. The provider can no longer bill Medicaid until re-enrollment is complete. Our team tracks revalidation dates and initiates the process in advance of deadlines to prevent any gap in enrollment status.

SUB_5_H3: Payer-Specific Nuances

PAYER_1_H4: Maryland Medical Assistance Program (MDH)
PAYER_1_BODY: Enrollment is completed through the Maryland Medical Assistance provider enrollment portal maintained by MDH. Providers must select the correct enrollment type before submission. An incorrect selection results in a denial and requires restarting the process.

PAYER_2_H4: HealthChoice Managed Care Plans
PAYER_2_BODY: After MDH enrollment is confirmed, providers who want to bill through HealthChoice must enroll separately with each MCO operating in the state, including Aetna Better Health of Maryland, Amerigroup Maryland, CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield Community Health Plan, Kaiser Permanente, Maryland Physicians Care, MedStar Family Choice, Priority Partners, and United Healthcare Community Plan of Maryland. These enrollments run on independent timelines and involve their own credentialing processes. Contracting Providers coordinates HealthChoice MCO enrollment following MDH confirmation so providers achieve full billing access as efficiently as possible.

PAYER_3_H4: NPI and Taxonomy Requirements
PAYER_3_BODY: Every Maryland Medical Assistance application requires a valid National Provider Identifier. For group practices, a Type 2 NPI is required in addition to each rendering provider's Type 1. Taxonomy codes must accurately reflect the provider's specialty. Mismatched taxonomy codes are a common cause of rejections and can set a provider back four to six weeks.

PAYER_4_H4: Disclaimer
PAYER_4_BODY: Contracting Providers is not MDH or any Maryland state agency. We help healthcare providers prepare, manage, and complete payer enrollment and credentialing tasks, including Maryland Medicaid provider enrollment support. MDH controls all final enrollment decisions and approvals.

## PROVIDER CREDENTIALING SECTION  (TEMPLATED)

SECTION_ID: provider-credentialing
H2: Provider Credentialing

PARAGRAPH_1: Maryland Medicaid provider enrollment and provider credentialing are closely linked but distinct processes. Enrollment grants a provider the right to bill MDH for Medical Assistance claims. Credentialing verifies that the provider meets the clinical and professional standards required by MDH and its HealthChoice managed care plans.
PARAGRAPH_2: HealthChoice MCOs require credentialing as a condition of in-network participation. Without credentialing, a provider enrolled at the state level cannot bill through the HealthChoice plans that serve the majority of Maryland Medicaid beneficiaries.

INVOLVES_H3: What Credentialing Involves
INVOLVES_ITEM_1: Primary source verification of Maryland medical licenses, DEA certificates, and board certifications
INVOLVES_ITEM_2: Review of malpractice history and National Practitioner Data Bank queries
INVOLVES_ITEM_3: Hospital affiliation verification where applicable
INVOLVES_ITEM_4: CAQH ProView data review and completion
INVOLVES_ITEM_5: HealthChoice MCO credentialing committee review and approval

TIMELINES_H3: Credentialing Timelines
TIMELINES_BODY: Credentialing through a HealthChoice MCO typically takes 60 to 120 days from initial application submission. Delays occur most commonly due to incomplete applications, outdated CAQH data, or unresolved primary source verification issues. Contracting Providers front-loads the process with a pre-submission audit to minimize avoidable delays.

BILLING_READINESS_H3: Credentialing and Billing Readiness
BILLING_READINESS_BODY: A provider who completes MDH enrollment but has not yet been credentialed by the relevant HealthChoice MCOs may face a gap period during which claims are denied. Our team coordinates enrollment and credentialing timelines simultaneously to reduce or eliminate that gap.

H2: FAQs and Resources

Q1: Do I need to enroll with Maryland Medicaid before billing Medicaid patients?
A1: Yes. Providers who want to order, refer, prescribe, or bill services for Maryland Medicaid recipients must be enrolled with MDH through the Medical Assistance Program before they can participate in the program. Q3: How long does Maryland Medicaid provider enrollment take?

Q8: Can you enroll providers in Maryland and other states at the same time?

Q9: Does Contracting Providers handle CHIP enrollment in Maryland?
